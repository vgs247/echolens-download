## What's new
- **Create your account in the app.** No website needed. Choose your username and go.
- **Buy time in the app.** Enter the time you want, see the price, pay, and send the screenshot to us in one click (WhatsApp, Telegram, Snapchat …). You can follow the status of your payment.
- **Smooth mode** (switch under your webcam) for slower PCs or internet connections: lighter video, fewer stutters.
- **Performance bar** while streaming shows *AI fps · Camera fps* with tips when the video is choppy.
- Security improvements.

## Install
1. Download **EchoLensSetup.exe** below.
2. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway** (EchoLens isn't code-signed yet).
3. Already installed? Just run the new installer. Your account and settings are kept.

**Requires Windows 11 (64-bit).** Restart your PC if *EchoLens Camera* doesn't appear in your video app.
