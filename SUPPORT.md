# Getting help

1. Read **Troubleshooting** in the [README](README.md#troubleshooting).
2. For camera problems, run **Start menu → EchoLens Camera Diagnostics**. It saves
   `EchoLens-diagnostics.txt` to your Desktop.
3. Contact us:
   - Email: **support@echolens.app**
   - Or use the contacts in the app under **Buy time**.

Always include your **username** and your Windows version. Never send your password.

Payment questions (time not added yet): send your **username** and the **payment screenshot**.
Time is normally added within about 30 minutes after we confirm the payment.
