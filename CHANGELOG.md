# Changelog

All notable changes to the EchoLens Windows app. Download the latest version from
[Releases](https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/echolens-download/releases/latest).

## 2.1.0
- **Create your account in the app.** Choose your username and you're ready to go.
- **Buy time in the app:** enter the time you want, see the price, pay, send the screenshot
  to us with one click (WhatsApp, Telegram, Snapchat …) and track your payment status.
- **Smooth mode** for slower PCs or internet: lighter video, fewer stutters.
- **Live performance bar** while streaming (AI fps · Camera fps) with tips when it's choppy.
- Security improvements.

## 2.0.4
- Fixed: *EchoLens Camera* showed no picture in Windows Settings and apps reported
  "camera not available".
- Fixed a crash when closing the app.
- Added **EchoLens Camera Diagnostics** to the Start menu.

## 2.0.0
- New EchoLens: one AI camera that works in Zoom, WhatsApp, Teams, Meet and more.
- Shows black (never your real webcam) whenever the AI isn't running.
- Countdown of your AI camera time with warnings at 5 and 1 minute.
